HOW TO PLAY:

  • A passage of Bible verses is chosen at random.
  • Words that have an antonym (opposite) may have been swapped. Each of these gets a drop-down so you can choose between the original word and its opposite.
  • Pick the words you think were in the original verse, then press Check to see what percent you have right.
  • Your goal is to restore the verse to its original message using as few checks as possible.
  • In the Daily Game, everyone gets the same passage each day, and you can share your results when you solve it.
  • I am not responsible for any heresies that generate on the game board.
